## Mechanism and measurement interpretation

- Cross-pass categories, counts and paired posthoc effects: cross-pass-*.csv/json. Distractor-only is distinct from both-pass or ordinary both-fail. Isolation and irrelevant controls address pre-existing distractor success.
- M1: m1-bundle-dependencies.json reports per-function and all-functions outcomes against component isolation, reused references and graph components. One connected membership component does not permit a robust independent-component CI.
- R1: r1-prompt-audit.csv records target repetition and preference presence. Oracle selection is privileged; formatting compaction is not semantic summarization. Token length/recency/instruction differences preclude a pure memory-policy mechanism.
- E5: e5-format-audit.csv and e5-joint-outcomes.csv retain original adherence. Fence-tolerant and comment/docstring-free proxies are posthoc, not independent human assessments of explanation content.
- Partial E4: repeated synthetic probes, not task samples; unmatched repeats are reported separately. The study is incomplete and constrained decoding changes its scope. Setup/priming energy is outside measured requests.

Energy tool/mode: HWiNFO CPU Package Power; trapezoidal; approximate post-load; block-start net. Condition totals are available-only and carry coverage; partial totals cannot be described as full condition costs. Task bootstrap reflects sampling variation, not sensor calibration. Signed net values remain signed. Conditions.csv/json include covered-population energy per correct and its denominator. No-correct ratios are undefined.

## Interpretation changers and unresolved limits

Reference/oracle and extraction discrepancies; caps and sandbox policy exclusions; missingness and selected eligibility; task overlap; instruction/length confounding; baseline drift/short sampling windows; benchmark exposure; two quantized models and one CPU. None of the observed contrasts measures cognition, intentional giving up or internal recognition. MemoryGraph has no evaluated intervention here.

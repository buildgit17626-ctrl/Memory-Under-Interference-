25% only for a uniformly random valid choice among four options. Competing
predictions are similar scores or lower recognition. Equal scores alone do
not identify a capacity limit; response format and mutant difficulty matter.

Use task bootstrap retaining the recognition and matched generation outcomes.

syntax, unequal option style, exposed canonical labels, training exposure,
invalid reference options or selection on generation failures can explain an
advantage. A positive gap describes performance across two different tasks;
it does not demonstrate hidden knowledge or intentional non-use.
